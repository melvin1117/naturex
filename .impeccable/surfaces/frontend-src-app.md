# Surface brief: NatureX prototype (Exchange, Steward, Control room, Judge)

Scope: every screen of the clickable prototype (ADR-0005 IA). Mode: Operate for Exchange, Steward and Control room; Persuade for /judge. Audience: a hackathon judge seeing NatureX cold (PRODUCT.md). Data: frontend-only scripted sandbox world seeded from real cached ratings (backend rate_site over demo_sites).

## Direction contract
THESIS: Living land as a calm, credible instrument: the site's own satellite image is the ground of every key card, numbers sit on it like a field instrument's readout. Refuses the trading-terminal default (dense red/green tickers, dark neon) and the eco-cliché (leaf icons, cream paper).
OWN-WORLD: Pinned by the user's references. Lichen-grey ground, off-white cards with 24–28px radii and soft offset shadows, deep forest-green ink and primary controls, lime and lilac as data colours, clay for pressure. Blurred real Sentinel-2 thumbnails as card grounds. A dark forest "machine layer" (pipeline, Agent Trace, ledger) with a dotted radial health instrument. Hanken Grotesk light numerals; Instrument Serif only for the wordmark and page titles.
STORY: The judge sees rated places, understands grade + range + confidence at a glance, backs a site, watches the Time Machine change it, asks why, and verifies the record.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Marketplace: page title left, sandbox totals right; a full-width feature card on the hero site's blurred image with grade stamp, dotted health ring, value range and stake price; site cards below. Primary action "Back this site" on the feature card.
FORM: Pinned brief (user references); no concept roll. Code-led.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
