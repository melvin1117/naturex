01

The fix

The rising lime stroke is gone and so is the colour: Bea is ink only. Her zigzag stripe now sits in the centre of her body, two carets side by side.

Before

A lime stroke climbing out of her stripe, ending in a dot. It looked like a chart tick, and it made Bea look like a second logo.

After

The same Bea, with no line and no colour. The zigzag stripe sits in the centre of her body.

02

Anatomy

Drawn by the logo's rules: one line weight with round ends, in ink only.

  1. Zigzag stripe. Two carets of the WildWorth line, centred on her body. It makes her a bee and makes her ours.
  2. Ink only. No fills and no colour: forest ink on light screens, paper on dark, like the logo's line.
  3. Wings and antennae. Outline only, same stroke. The antennae carry her mood: up when alert, wiggling while she thinks, drooping when she's asleep.
  4. Face. Dot eyes and a line smile, shared with the brand's other characters. No blush, no extra colour.
  5. Build. 120-unit square, 6-unit stroke. Body circle 33 units; the stripe is clipped inside it.
03

States

Each expression has a job in the product. Bea never shows a face that doesn't match what's happening on screen.

Motion

Small, constant life at rest; one clear reaction when a rating changes. Everything stops for anyone who prefers reduced motion.

Idle

Wings flutter, she bobs gently, and she blinks every few seconds.

Loop · 3.6 s bob · 0.32 s flutter

Thinking

While the model is answering: antennae wiggle and three dots pulse beside her.

Loop until the answer arrives

Upgrade

A hop with a little squash, and her smile.

750 ms · once

Downgrade

A short shake, worried eyes and raised antennae. Never cartoon panic.

600 ms · once

Easing: cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), the app's --nx-ease-out. Try every state on the stage at the top of this page.

07

Voice

Bea is warm in tone and strict about facts. The personality changes how things are said, never what the numbers are.

Rules

  • “Bzz” only to open a first answer, never mid-sentence or twice in a row.
  • Every number comes from the Record, with its source tag.
  • If the Record has no answer, she says so and suggests what to check.
  • Never invest, returns, buy or profit. Play money is play money.
  • In reports and the Agent Trace, the sober rating voice leads; Bea is labelled with the model she runs on.

Say it like this

✓Bzz. The satellites saw more water this quarter, so the score rose to 77.6.
✕Great news!!! This wetland is a fantastic opportunity! 🐝
✓I couldn't find that in the Record. The latest news check was 12 Sep.
✕I think it's probably fine, most wetlands recover.
