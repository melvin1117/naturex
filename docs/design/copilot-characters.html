Who answers when you ask why?

Nine characters for the assistant that explains a site's rating: Sprig, the gentle tree you asked for, the first four, and four new ones. All are drawn by the logo's rules and all move. Each has an idle loop, blinks, and reacts when a rating changes. Try the Downgrade and Upgrade buttons on each card.

One line weightEvery stroke is the same weight with round ends, like the logo's line.
Ink onlyForest ink on light grounds, paper on dark. No pinks, browns or blush.
One lime accentLime-ink on light, lime on dark. Each character's accent means something.
Motion that means somethingThe lime part is what moves: it draws, glows or rises, like the logo's reading.
